The Purple Moon

The Purple Moon is a “fantasy of pure enlightenment, but then some may say it is reality.” In this grand and enchanting land the humble spirit is taken on a journey it did not know existed.

The spirit is embraced real tight by the glorious light, and in this light it is introduced to hope, peace and delight. In this grand purple land the humble little spirit is completely free, and sees eternal life with many possibilities.

Come in the purple land to embrace your light. Hold on, you will be alright.

About Deidre

I’m a graduate of the Art Institute in Atlanta, Georgia, with an advertising design degree. I was born in Norwalk, Connecticut, in 1961 and I’ve been drawing for as long as I can remember. I run Aringa Creations: home, décor and more for your body, mind and soul.

“I believe in living life to the fullest and constantly evolving to your higher self and remaining humble and thankful for all that life has to offer. My mission is to spread the light.”
